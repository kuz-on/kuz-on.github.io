About

I build the parts of a game players never see, and the parts they touch every minute.

I got into programming in sixth grade, starting with C++ before I found game development. I tried a few different paths, and Roblox Studio is where scripting finally clicked. Since then I’ve spent over 3.5 years learning, building and taking commissions, and I’ve picked up a better feel for both the platform and the people making games on it.

When I work with clients, I care about the idea behind a request, not only about making the code work. I keep communication clear, show progress as I go, and build systems that fit the game they’re made for.

FAQ

What do I need from you to start?

A short description of what you want, when you need it and your budget range. If it has to fit an existing game, access to it (Team Create or a copy of the place) helps.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. You get a timeline in the plan, and I tell you early if anything shifts.

How does payment work?

The price is in the plan I send you. Payment is settled when you approve the work, and the finished script is delivered as soon as it clears.

Can you work with my existing game and code?

Yes. I read through what’s already there first and write to match its structure, so new systems fit in instead of fighting the old ones.

Will my data and economy be safe from exploiters?

Anything players shouldn’t be able to change, like currency and inventories, is handled and checked on the server. I also test performance before handing a system over.

What if I want changes?

A round of fixes is included at delivery. Bigger changes get their own quote.

What if something breaks after delivery?

I fix problems with what I delivered at no charge, for a set period after delivery that’s stated in the plan.

Who owns the script?

You do. Once it’s delivered, it’s yours to use in your game.
